- `MetadataCompileResult` now includes required numeric `tokenCalibration`, the isolated metadata-view calibration used for its canonical `estimatedTokens` total. Consumers can price selected content and unresolved provenance in the same units through `ContextManager.estimateContentTokens(content, calibration)`, which delegates to the existing owned MessageStore estimator, preserving custom text estimation, stamped/signed/media prices and per-block rounding without constructing stores, registering indexes or changing live calibration. Dry metadata reads still leave render state, compression, persistence and ordinary compile behavior unchanged.
