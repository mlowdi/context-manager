- `MetadataCompileResult` now includes required numeric `tokenCalibration`, the isolated metadata-view calibration used for its canonical `estimatedTokens` total. Consumers can price selected content and unresolved provenance in the same units through `ContextManager.estimateContentTokens(content, calibration)`, which delegates to the existing owned MessageStore estimator, preserving custom text estimation, stamped/signed/media prices and per-block rounding without constructing stores, registering indexes or changing live calibration. Dry metadata reads still leave render state, compression, persistence and ordinary compile behavior unchanged.
- `compileMetadata(budget, { provenance: true })` returns the exported `MetadataCompileResultWithProvenance` with a required data-only `provenance` snapshot: Chronicle `branch` identity (`id`, `name`, `head`), normalized-message-aligned `entries` (`renderedTokens`, raw-leaf `sourceMessageIds`, exact `sourceSummaryIds`, `summaryLevel`), and referenced-original `sources` (`id`, canonical `tokens`, detached `timestamp`). Rendered prices are computed once and sum to `estimatedTokens`; originals are priced once from the same unresolved own+auxiliary merged population, before boolean selection filtering. Default result/message shapes and their work remain unchanged.
- Recall answers now carry ephemeral exact summary identities across adaptive per-summary, hierarchical positioned and legacy combined emission. Snapshot capture expands their level-relative forests with cycle/leaf deduplication; questions and generated tool scaffolding remain unattributed. No source views, callbacks or original payloads escape, no indexes are registered, and diagnostic selection does not publish render/calibration/fold state. Dry-run plan/cache/KV-escalation reports are silent; ordinary compile plan/escalation diagnostics and stats remain intact.
